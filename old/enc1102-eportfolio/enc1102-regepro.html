research genre production

Students will be able to produce writing that demonstrates their ability to navigate choices and constraints in a variety of public and/or academic research genres that matter to specific communities.

Artifact 1 - Project 2: Draft 1

Project 2 is a great example of writing in a research genre where I can pick out specific choices that were made and some constraints I had to follow.

This assignment asks us to find a research question that centers around online communities and seek that answer out with scholarly sources to back up primary data and analysis.

In this artifact, I wrote about a question regarding free digital labor in a specific niche. To accomplish this, I made it clear that this covers a gap in research and focused the research on covering that gap.

By focusing the research in on the niche, I made a choice that works into this genre. It also creates constraints that I work around, like previous research.

I addressed this constraint in the literature review portion, where I go over previous research done in the field of study and what should be known before entering the niche of research that will be covered.
