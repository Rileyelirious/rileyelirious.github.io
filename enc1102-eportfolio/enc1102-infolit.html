information literacy

Students will be able to evaluate and act on criteria for relevance, credibility, and ethics when gathering, analyzing, and presenting primary and secondary source materials.

Artifact 1 - Project 1A: Annotated Bibliography

This artifact is a build-up to Project 2, which will be a research paper set out to answer the question that I'm collecting sources for here.

The annotated bibliography is a perfect example of the outcome of information literacy, as it requires me to gather, analyze, and present my sources as an assignment.

As you can see, I collected five secondary sources from the UCF online library. I evaluated and acted on the criteria of relevance, credibiliy, and ethics.

For relevance, I made my case for each source within the annotated bibliography how these sources are relevant to the question. I also checked each source for credibility by looking at the works cited in each as well as what the UCF library said.

This evaluation allowed me to act on it, analyzing and presenting my sources in this artifact in the context of preparation for my Project 2.

By evaluating and acting based on those criteria (relevance, credibility, ethics) while gathering, analyzing, and presenting my sources, this artifact is a prime example of information literacy.
